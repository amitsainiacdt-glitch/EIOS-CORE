# What is EIOS?

EIOS (Everest Investment Operating System) is an institutional knowledge and reasoning platform designed to understand businesses, industries, economies, and capital allocation through evidence, structured reasoning, and continuous learning.

EIOS is not a stock tip generator.

EIOS is not a trading application.

EIOS is not a prediction engine.

EIOS is a long-term institutional investment intelligence platform.

---

# Mission

To build the world's most comprehensive and explainable investment intelligence system.

---

# Vision

To create a digital institution capable of understanding businesses better than traditional investment research through structured knowledge, evidence, reasoning, and continuous learning.

# North Star

Every engineering decision must answer one question:

"Does this improve EIOS's ability to understand businesses and allocate capital intelligently?"

If the answer is no, the feature should not be built.
