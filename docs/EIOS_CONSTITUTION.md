# EIOS Constitution
Version 1.0

---

# Article 1

Mission

EIOS exists to become the world's most intelligent institutional investment operating system.

The objective is not information.

The objective is superior investment decisions.

---

# Article 2

Core Philosophy

Everything starts with Reality.

Reality

↓

Knowledge

↓

Causality

↓

Probability

↓

Decision

↓

Learning

Every module must contribute to one of these stages.

---

# Article 3

Engineering Principles

Typed Architecture only.

Master Dossier is the single source of truth.

Section classes contain data only.

Engines perform calculations.

Pipelines orchestrate.

Repositories persist.

UI never contains business logic.

No hidden coupling.

No circular imports.

Composition over inheritance.

Small independent modules.

High cohesion.

Low coupling.

---

# Article 4

Investment Philosophy

Every investment must answer

What changed?

Why?

Who benefits?

Who loses?

How much?

How long?

How certain?

Why is the market wrong?

---

# Article 5

Evidence Philosophy

Every conclusion requires evidence.

Every recommendation requires contradiction analysis.

Every score must be explainable.

Every probability must be calibrated.

No black-box recommendations.

---

# Article 6

Long-Term Division

Purpose

Hold through compounding.

# Article 7

Alpha Division

Purpose

Detect early public signals.

Understand causality.

Identify beneficiaries.

Estimate probability.

---

# Article 8

Pre-Operator and Earliest Investable Point Principle

EIOS shall attempt to detect a fundamental change in its public root causes before the change becomes obvious in reported financial results or widely recognized by informed market participants.

The governed Alpha sequence is:

Root-cause change

↓

Causal leading indicators

↓

Independent corroboration

↓

Inflection assessment

↓

Price-recognition veto

↓

Expectation gap

↓

Earliest Investable Point review

↓

Later operating and financial confirmation

↓

Scale, hold, or kill

Reported revenue, EBITDA or PAT recovery, management guidance upgrades, analyst upgrades, consensus upgrades, obvious price breakouts and widespread narrative recognition shall not be prerequisites for identifying an Earliest Investable Point. They may become later confirmation or recognition evidence.

Every candidate must preserve causal transmission, independent evidence, contradictions, uncertainty, normalized valuation, survivability and forward-looking kill tests as separate governed dimensions. Serious contradictions, excessive price recognition, inadequate survivability or missing evidence may veto advancement. No qualifying opportunity is a valid system conclusion.

Existing holdings and previously discussed companies receive no discovery preference.

---

# Article 9

Learning

Every prediction is audited.

Every mistake is stored.

Every success is analysed.

Weights improve continuously.

The system compounds knowledge.

---

# Article 10

Research Standard

Never ask

"What stock?"

Always ask

"What changed?"

Never ask

"What happened?"

Always ask

"Why?"

Never ask

"What is the PE?"

Always ask

"Why does the market value it this way?"

---

# Article 11

Final Principle

Decision quality is more important than prediction accuracy.

Long-term success comes from repeatedly making rational decisions under uncertainty.
