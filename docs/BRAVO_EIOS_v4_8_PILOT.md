# BRAVO EIOS v4.8 — Experimental Discovery & Decision Pilot
Status: PILOT, not deployed. v4.7 remains active and its thresholds unchanged.

## Discovery first
Screen three independent lanes before familiar stocks: adjusted 30–60% distressed-quality drawdowns, operating repair (capacity, orders, spreads, dispatch, collections), and completed asset monetisation/debt repair. Apply eight causal engines and a ninth verified Alpha-EIOS superinvestor deal check. Do not mistake secondary transactions, warrants, dilution, old holdings or investor names for a buy thesis. Report screened universe, false positives, early rejections and research limits. Missing historical statistics are NOT MEASURED, not zero.

An INVESTIGATE file needs a dated source, what broke, a falsifiable repair mechanism, an early causal signal and the next 2–4-quarter trigger; it does not need perfect trailing cash flow or a buy price.

## Decision completion
For a research ADD, require verified primary filings (links alone are not verification), two corroborated dated signals or documented exceptional equivalent; FY27–FY29 normalised attributable earnings and fully diluted shares; reverse price expectations; bear/base/bull with justified probabilities; cash-flow, project funding, credit, governance, dilution and downside audit. Apply v4.7 minimum 18–20% base CAGR and 2:1 upside/downside preferences; document exceptions explicitly. Weak explainable cash flow is not an automatic veto; unfinanceable debt, opaque material dilution, credible fatal governance contradictions are hard blocks. WAIT must name specific blocker, next evidence and review date. No ADD quota or fabricated price zone.

## Anti-hindsight
Date-freeze research evidence at each historical cutoff; prohibit later filings/prices in decision input. Future returns can only be outcomes. Compare eligible vs rejected cases over 3/6/12 months, with dates, adjusted prices, original verdicts, denominator and predetermined benchmark. Do not count misses or false positives without this evidence.
