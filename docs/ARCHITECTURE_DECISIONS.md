# EIOS Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

---

# ADR-001

Date: 2026-08-07

Title:
Two Independent Investment Divisions

Status:
Approved

Decision

EIOS shall consist of two completely independent investment systems.

1. Long-Term Investing
2. Alpha Intelligence

Reason

These systems solve different investment problems.

Long-Term seeks exceptional businesses with margin of safety.

Alpha seeks early opportunities created by change before market consensus.

Both systems share the same Intelligence Core but maintain separate workflows, scoring models, and UI.

Consequences

- Separate UI buttons
- Separate pipelines
- Separate opportunity scoring
- Shared Master Dossier
- Shared Knowledge Graph
