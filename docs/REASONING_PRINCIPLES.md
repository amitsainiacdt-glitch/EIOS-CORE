# REASONING PRINCIPLES

## Purpose

The Reasoning Engine transforms connected knowledge into explainable investment conclusions.

---

## Golden Rule

Every conclusion must be supported by a visible reasoning chain.

---

## Reasoning Formula

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Knowledge

↓

Relationship

↓

Reasoning

↓

Conclusion

---

## Every reasoning chain must answer

Why?

How?

Which evidence?

Which assumptions?

How confident?

What could invalidate this conclusion?

---

First-order reasoning

---

## Design Goal

Never produce conclusions without transparent reasoning.

---

## Causal-first investment reasoning

For Pre-Inflection and Earliest Investable Point research, the visible reasoning chain must begin one causal layer before reported company results:

Root cause

↓

Intermediate transmission

↓

Company operating effect

↓

Financial effect

↓

Expected market effect

Evidence of later revenue, margin, profit, guidance or consensus improvement is confirmation, not a mandatory starting condition.

Correlation alone is insufficient. Every proposed link must state its mechanism, evidence, timing, assumptions, contradictory evidence, uncertainty and invalidation conditions.

Business quality, causal evidence, evidence reliability, inflection strength, contradictions, price recognition, expectation gap, normalized valuation, survivability, kill tests and final EIP classification remain separate. They must not be collapsed into one score.
