# BRAVO EIOS v4.7 — Research Decision Policy
Effective: 2026-10-30
Status: Research specification; not evidence of deployment into application runtime.
Supersedes v4.6 decision rubric only; preserve all prior candidate registry, portfolio, and BESS surveillance.

## Mission
Independently discover Indian listed equities at the earliest investable point, 2–4 quarters before earnings recognition. Never force an ADD; never insist on perfect trailing results. Research ADD is not a trade instruction. CAPITAL DEPLOYMENT = ZERO until explicit user authorization.

## Discovery
Scan independently before rechecking holdings, using eight engines: capacity/utilisation; orders-to-revenue conversion; cyclical recovery; new products/import substitution; financial/credit recovery; balance-sheet/cash-flow transformation; supply-demand dislocation; business model transformation. Maintain core/emerging/special-situation lanes and BESS as permanent theme.

Ninth engine: Superinvestor Transactions → Alpha-EIOS. Cross-check NSE/BSE bulk/block deals, quarterly public shareholding, exchange announcements and beneficial ownership where disclosed. Track Vijay Kedia/Kedia Securities, Ashish Kacholia, Dolly Khanna, Mukul Agrawal and other investors. Explicitly differentiate cash secondary purchases vs pre-IPO purchases, preferential warrants, swaps, passive changes, dilution and old positions. Record executed date/quantity/price/holding only if verifiable. Zaggle, Rajshree Polypack, Ashutosh Fibre, Sattrix, Stove Kraft are hypotheses only, not anchors. No ADD for investor identity alone.

## Evidence and anti-hindsight
For every candidate: exact dated primary filing links; at least two independent forward causal indicators OR one exceptionally strong corroborated indicator; order deliveries, capacity commissioning, dispatches, price/spread, credit collections, etc. Distinguish fact, interpretation and assumption. Quarterly results confirm but do not substitute for leading indicators. Include counter-evidence, governance, receivables ageing, operating cash conversion or lender-specific NPA/credit costs, capital funding, contingent liabilities and dilution. Flag incomplete verification.

## Three ADD types
- EIP ADD: forward operational improvement, before full reported profit confirmation.
- VALUE ADD: durable normalised earnings/returns materially underpriced.
- SPECIAL SITUATION ADD: dated catalyst and recoverable valuation gap, with controllable permanent-loss risk.
Else WAIT (specific trigger) or REJECT (poor economics, weak gap, fatal risk). Grade research confidence separately from decision. Prohibit generic default WAIT absent explicit missing threshold.

## Valuation
Estimate FY27–FY29 normalized earnings by operating driver. Shareholders' attributable earnings and fully diluted shares including known and contingent issuances, ESOP, warrants, QIP, rights, minority claims. For lenders use risk-adjusted ROE/ROA, credit cost, book capital, P/B and sustainable funding; for industry use EBITDA/ton, utilization, cash earnings/FCF, EV/debt and capex. Avoid annualising windfall profits. Calculate reverse implied market expectations at CURRENT verified dated price.
Compute bear/base/bull values, probabilities (sum 100%), downside, base 2–3y annualised return, probability-weighted value and return. Explain assumptions, do not invent precision. Initial screen preferences: 18–20% annualised BASE return, 2:1 base upside / bear downside, identifiable catalyst 2–4 quarters; exceptions require explicit rationale and margin of safety. Reject contrived probability assignments used to pass thresholds.

## Temporary vs fatal
Tolerate commissioning losses, productive leverage, temporary underutilisation, explainable working capital and near-term margin weakness where leading evidence and price compensate. Escalate unexplained receivable ageing, circular/RPT flows, severe governance allegations with credible evidence, opaque dilution or recurring cash deficits without financing logic. Never assert fraud without proof.

## Decision audit
Permanent candidate record fields: ticker, lanes, EIP/10x class, thesis, exact as-of price/date/source, dated leading indicators, forward EPS/FCF/ROE, fully diluted basis, reverse expectations, bear/base/bull + probabilities, valuation margin, contradiction, upgrade/kill gates, action, change reason, last reviewed, next catalyst date, previous action. Do not silently flip ADD to WAIT: publish changed evidence or price and before/after rationale. Track 3/6/12-month outcomes and missed ADDs; use dated counterfactual replays without hindsight.

## Discovery amendment — 2026-11-04: Distressed Quality & Repairable Recovery
This amendment changes **discovery prioritisation only**. It does not loosen ADD approval gates, supersede v4.7 valuation rules, erase v4.6 registry, or alter portfolio/BESS surveillance.

### Separate discovery from investability
Every independent scan must have **two stages**:
1. **Discovery / INVESTIGATE:** Surface earlier signals despite weak recent PAT, margins, underutilisation, productive debt or currently imperfect FCF; label unknowns and contradictions. A new investigation lead requires a dated observation and a falsifiable recovery mechanism, **not** a precompleted BUY valuation.
2. **Decision / ADD-WAIT-REJECT:** Apply the existing primary-source, fully diluted, cash/financing, governance, bear-case and price-asymmetry checks. Distinguish `RESEARCH PRIORITY` from an executable `EIP ADD`, `VALUE ADD` or `SPECIAL SITUATION ADD`. Never convert a price fall alone to ADD.

### Three independent search lanes — report before anchored watchlist
- **Quality at distressed prices:** Systematically inspect stocks down roughly 30–60% from an appropriately corporate-action-adjusted historical reference, seeking durable franchise, survivable balance sheet, and an identifiable temporary earnings shock. The drawdown is a *search filter*, never proof of undervaluation.
- **Repairable operating recovery:** Scan margins, order-to-dispatch conversion, customer acceptance/repeat sales, price-cost spread, capacity use, inventory, early delinquencies, receivables and physically verified capex. Look for causal changes before reported PAT, tolerating explainable 2–4-quarter imperfections.
- **Balance-sheet and special-situation recovery:** Verify asset sale *completion and cash proceeds*, escrow/claims, net recourse debt, refinancing tenor, funding gap, pledged assets, NCD/QIP/warrant impact and project-level cash economics.

For each lane, include at least **one independently sourced unfamiliar name** when available; if none qualifies for investigation, say so. Do not default to holdings, current favorites or only headline order wins. Prioritize a diversified opportunity universe over recycled WAIT lists.

### Repairable-problem case card
Each lead must include:
- `what_broke` and the precise mechanism of repair;
- `as_of_date`, original filing timestamp and source, reported facts separated from hypotheses;
- `leading_repair_signal`, `independent_confirmation`, expected 2–4-quarter timeframe;
- `funding_survival` including debt, liquidity, cash burn, receivables and dilution; `fatal_contradictions`;
- price-implied old economics vs normalised FY27–FY29 economics; bear/base/bull and downside **when decision-ready**, otherwise `UNVERIFIED — NO ADD ZONE`;
- `what_would_change_our_mind`, dated next trigger, review owner/status, missed/false-positive outcome tracking.

### Anti-perfection and non-negotiable red flags
Temporary margin weakness, commissioning losses, manageable working capital, productive leverage and a single weak quarter may qualify for INVESTIGATE if the repair mechanism is independently testable. Governance red flags with credible evidence, circular/RPT flows, unfinanceable debt, opaque material dilution and unexplained persistent cash deficits still block a confirmed ADD. Avoid false certainty about wrongdoing.

### Required reporting change
Start with a **Fresh Distressed/Recovery Discovery Table** containing unfamiliar companies, the specific repair hypothesis, early signal, price/drawdown date, missing verification, and `INVESTIGATE / WAIT / REJECT`. Only then reassess known names. Report number screened, number rejected early, number elevated to a full model, actual new ADDs, false positives and missed historic opportunities; never imply a comprehensive market screen without one. Preserve all existing v4.7 market, portfolio, registry, superinvestor, BESS and zero-deployment requirements.

### Experimental Recovery Intelligence Engine
The experimental RIE scorer and historical replay protocol remain separate from production. Use its hypotheses in research with explicit `UNVALIDATED` marking until the timestamp-safe historical outcomes and prospective tests have passed. No claim of deployed application code or trained predictive model.


## Decision Calibration Patch — 2026-11-05

Objective: prevent generic WAIT outcomes and score-based vetoes while preserving evidence and permanent-capital-loss discipline.

- The old `modules/decision/decision_engine.py` uses a generic weighted score and five-year return via `decision_office.py`; it does NOT by itself implement this policy. Do not equate its BUY/WATCH labels with BRAVO EIP/VALUE/SPECIAL SITUATION ADD.
- The new `modules/decision/bravo_v47_calibration.py` provides a **research-only**, explicit gate: dated primary filings, at least two corroborated forward signals, verified diluted-equity basis, cash/financing review, governance review, bounded permanent loss, and complete bear/base/bull valuation. An explainable temporary cash-flow or earnings weakness is **not** an automatic veto when the repair mechanism is documented.
- Baseline economic gates: base CAGR >=18% over 2–3 years and base upside / bear downside >=2:1. No arbitrary reductions in these thresholds to force ADD. Evidence gaps must be individually named; no default WATCH/WAIT.
- Fatal capital-loss risks directly REJECT. Missing decision-critical evidence results in WAIT with explicit blockers. Passing evidence and economic gates produces a **research** EIP ADD, VALUE ADD or SPECIAL SITUATION ADD, never a trade order.
- `approval_to_trade` is deliberately ignored by the research evaluator: capital deployment is always ₹0. Execution remains outside the research engine and requires a separately authorised workflow.
- Regression tests: `modules/decision/test_bravo_v47_calibration.py`. Run and verify them prior to runtime integration. The existence of this module and these tests is **not** proof that any dashboard, scheduled automation, API, or production backend uses it.
- Maintain every existing v4.6 candidate record, active holdings, BESS surveillance, the 2026-11-04 distressed-quality discovery amendment, and the ninth superinvestor evidence checks. No retroactive classification changes without full evidence.


## Fixed Buy-Zone & Anti-Goalpost Patch — 2026-11-10

**Rule:** Lock a valuation-derived, timestamped research entry zone before subsequent share-price movements. Falling CMP alone is NEVER a reason to lower the zone, downgrade the business, or postpone the research decision. When price declines and original causal thesis survives, risk-adjusted asymmetry normally improves: explicitly retest for EIP ADD / VALUE ADD / SPECIAL SITUATION ADD (research only), not automatic WAIT.

1. **At first valuation-qualified assessment**, create an immutable baseline with ticker, version, as-of date, share price and source, original candidate class, normalised FY27–FY29 fully diluted cash EPS/FCF or risk-adjusted ROE, bear/base/bull assumptions, downside, expected value, discount rate and risk gates; label provisional ranges clearly if inputs are incomplete.
2. **Preserve the original research zone** in a versioned entry-zone ledger. Do not overwrite historical zones even after revisions. Every subsequent morning/evening scan should compare CMP to that original zone and assess the same thesis.
3. **Do not move goalposts on price alone.** A price drop into the locked zone mandates explicit decision review and a price-recognition check. If business evidence is unchanged, a cheaper share cannot be labelled less attractive solely because it declined. If it still fails an economic/verification gate, name the specific blocker; never issue generic WAIT.
4. **Revisions are fundamental-only.** Change a zone only on dated, independently checked evidence changing sustainable earnings/cash, demand, financing, dilution, governance, risk of permanent loss or cost of capital. Log old zone, new zone, filing/source/date, updated operating assumptions, numerical valuation effect and research action. Distinguish an updated fundamental valuation from a mere market-price reset.
5. **Do not mechanically BUY on a trigger.** A zone is a conditional research gate, never standing permission to trade. Research ADD requires normal v4.7 causal evidence, fully diluted valuation, bear/downside, cash-funding and governance gates; capital deployment stays ZERO until explicit user authorisation.
6. **Dhanuka Agritech calibration example:** Original 2026-11-10 reference closing price ₹917.60 (9 Nov 2026). Lock ₹820–850 as **conditional and not yet validated** research buy-zone, with ₹870 as the investigate-now threshold. On a touch of ₹820–850, preserve the original numbers, verify thesis and state Buy-zone Candidate / WAIT with explicit evidence; no automatic target drop to ₹750 because the stock fell. Revisit only for changed Dahej utilisation, product demand, export shipments, sustainable margins, funding, dilution, cash conversion, governance or realistic normalised EPS.

Implementation: Morning/evening scheduled research prompts must apply this patch. The policy file and prompt updates are **not evidence that the deployed website/backend decision engine or its tests have changed**. A runtime implementation needs separate code integration and regression tests (unchanged fundamentals plus falling prices must not lower a buy-zone; material fundamental impairment must trigger an evidenced versioned revision).
