Every software module, every Python class and every future feature must fit within this architecture.

# Core Layers

## Layer 1

Observation

Purpose

Observe the world without interpretation.

Examples

- Annual Reports
- Quarterly Results
- Orders
- News
- Weather
- Commodity Prices

---

## Layer 2

Evidence

Purpose

Convert observations into verified evidence.

---

## Layer 3

Knowledge

Purpose

Organize verified evidence into structured knowledge.

---

## Layer 4

Models

Purpose

Represent how businesses and economies work.

Examples

- Business Model
- Industry Model
- Macro Model
- Financial Model

---

## Layer 5

Intelligence

Purpose

Generate domain intelligence.

Examples

- Company Intelligence
- Macro Intelligence
- Industry Intelligence
- Financial Intelligence

---

## Layer 6

Reasoning

Purpose

Connect intelligence into conclusions.

Capabilities

- Causal Reasoning
- Scenario Analysis
- Confidence Assessment
- Opportunity Cost

---

## Layer 7

Decision

Purpose

Support institutional investment decisions.

---

## Layer 8

Portfolio

Purpose

Allocate capital.

---

## Layer 9

Learning

Purpose

Continuously improve EIOS.

---

# Core Principles

1. Evidence before opinion.

2. Understanding before conviction.

3. Every decision must be explainable.

4. Every module has one responsibility.

5. Documentation is part of the software.

6. Continuous learning is mandatory.
