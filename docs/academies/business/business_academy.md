# EIOS Business Academy

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

The Business Academy is the foundational knowledge system of EIOS.

Its purpose is to teach EIOS how businesses create, sustain, and compound value.

Every business-related engine, model, and Master Dossier section derives its knowledge from this academy.

The Business Academy is the primary source of institutional business knowledge.
---

# Learning Objectives

After completing the Business Academy, EIOS should be able to:

- Explain how a business creates value.
- Identify business models.
- Evaluate competitive advantages.
- Understand industry economics.
- Assess scalability.
- Identify business quality.
- Recognize structural risks.
- Build an evidence-based business assessment.
