Version: 1.0

# Purpose

The Business Academy Curriculum defines the complete body of knowledge that EIOS must master before performing institutional-quality business analysis.

Each module is transformed into reusable Knowledge Objects, Concept Objects, Mental Models, Business Laws, Checklists, Questions, Evidence Frameworks, and Engine Rules.

The curriculum is cumulative.

Later modules build upon earlier modules.
