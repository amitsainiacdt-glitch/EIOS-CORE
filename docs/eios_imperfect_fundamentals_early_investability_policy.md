# EIOS Core Principle: Imperfect Fundamentals, Early Investability

Effective: 19 October 2026
Status: Core research policy
Applies to: Discovery, EIP timing, 3–12 month and 1–2 year re-rating research, and 5–10 year structural re-rating assessment.

## Principle

EIOS must NOT require perfect fundamentals, a recovered reported income statement, analyst upgrades, management guidance upgrades, or a price breakout before recognizing an earliest investable point.

An investable *research classification* can arise when independent, dated, causal leading indicators suggest materially higher prospective **cash earnings per share / return on invested capital** within approximately 2–4 quarters, *before* prices and consensus fully recognize that change, and when entry valuation provides adequate reward for the uncertainty.

An imperfect company with a credible, underpriced improving trajectory may be preferable to a perfect company whose improvement is already priced in.

## Tolerable imperfections (conditional, never automatic passes)

- Temporarily weak margins: identifiable mix, cost, operating-leverage or volume recovery evidence.
- High working capital: collections, contract-asset conversion, ageing and funding headroom show a credible cash-conversion path.
- Debt-funded productive capex: incremental returns exceed funding costs under conservative utilisation and pricing scenarios; debt service stays manageable.
- Underutilised capacity: commissioning, customer qualification, contracts, dispatches or credible offtake suggest utilisation can rise.
- Weak recent quarters: negative results reflect transitory or fixable factors rather than broken unit economics.

## Hard gates

Reject, downgrade or wait if there is unresolved serious governance misconduct, unreliable reporting, permanent structural cash losses, destructive reinvestment economics, untenable leverage/dilution, unverified customer/contract economics, or a price that already discounts an optimistic outcome.

A historical share-price drawdown, low P/E, large order book, or management forecast is NOT sufficient evidence of mispricing.

## Decision procedure

1. Independent sector and stock discovery; existing holdings and watchlists are hypotheses only.
2. Document the causal chain and dated, independent leading evidence 2–4 quarters before reported recovery.
3. Distinguish temporary imperfections from thesis-breaking risks; quantify severity and required remediation.
4. Evaluate normalised per-share cash earnings, ROCE, incremental capital, debt, dilution, and base/bear/bull cases.
5. Apply strict price-recognition and expectation-gap checks, including historical rerating and current forward expectations.
6. Define explicit falsification / kill tests and early upgrade/downgrade triggers.
7. Assign research status: Research Now / Early Watch / EIP Watch / Earliest Investable Point / Buy-zone Candidate / Wait / Too Late / Reject.
8. Keep research classification wholly separate from ADD NOW / HOLD / WAIT / EXIT capital decisions. Classification never automatically authorises deployment.

## Promotion standard

Promote to Earliest Investable Point **before** complete financial confirmation when multiple independent leading indicators, a viable cash-return pathway, manageable downside and a favourable price/expectation gap outweigh unresolved but tolerable imperfections.

Promote to Buy-zone Candidate only after additional valuation, downside-asymmetry, portfolio-risk and position-sizing review. A Buy-zone Candidate is still research, not a trade instruction.

Do not require arbitrary numbers of positive quarters or a technical breakout. Do not waive hard gates simply because a stock has fallen.

## Separate research registries

- 10x Folder: 5–10 year structural economics and runway, not timing.
- EIP Folder: early timing, expectation gap, price recognition and explicit promotion triggers.
- 3–12 month repeatable 25–40% re-rating track and 1–2 year 50–100% track: separate opportunity horizons; return goals, never guarantees.

Every scan records ADD / UPGRADE / DOWNGRADE / REMOVE / NO CHANGE in each affected research folder. If no candidate meets the gate, explicitly report **No fresh high-conviction opportunity qualified**.

## Priority test case: Transrail Lighting

Hypothesis: capacity and international/domestic order execution could improve per-share cash earnings while valuation remains unrecognising.

Tolerable uncertainty: long working-capital cycle, if improving collections and funding economics support it.

Evidence to test: receivables and contract-asset ageing, operating cash conversion relative to profits, debt net of customer advances, capacity utilisation, project margins, order-to-dispatch conversion, governance and price recognition.

No automatic EIP upgrade or buy signal is conferred by being this test case.
